Write it down. Then let it go.

You don't have to remember where you left anything. Each task carries its own notes and its own next steps, so picking it up again takes a glance.

  1. 1

    Jot it down

    Add a task the way you'd say it: "find a plumber for the kitchen sink" or "prep for Thursday's review".

  2. 2

    Its helper gets going

    The task's helper researches, compares, drafts and writes down what it found and what comes next.

  3. 3

    You make the calls

    When it needs you, the question comes with the answers laid out. One click, and it carries on.

Questions arrive with the answers ready.

Open a task and you'll find a short summary, what's been done and the next steps. Steps are a click away, and decisions come as choices, not homework.

  • Tick a step and the helper takes it from there
  • Steps wait their turn until the one before is done
  • Talk to the helper on the task's page whenever you like

Mornings start sorted.

An assistant looks after the list as a whole. Ask it to triage, reshuffle or hand work to a task's helper, or give it a routine and it shows up on its own.

  • Routines follow your work hours: start of the day, every hour while you work, or whenever you choose
  • Several conversations can run side by side
  • It works with the tools you've connected to Claude Code, like your calendar, mail and docs

It gets to know how you like things done.

Knowledge is a set of notes about you, your people and your projects that every helper reads before it starts. Flagship can build it for you from the tools you've connected, and you can edit any of it.

Quick notes have a home too. Jot things down as they come; helpers search them when it matters and leave them be otherwise.

Questions

What do I need to use Flagship?

A Mac on macOS 26 or later, and Claude Code signed in to your Claude account. Flagship's helpers run on Claude Code, so they use your existing Claude plan.

Will it do things without asking me?

Reading and preparing are fine. Anything that leaves your Mac or speaks for you, like sending an email, posting, accepting an invite or buying something, waits for your go-ahead. When in doubt, it drafts and shows you first.

Where does my list live?

On your Mac. Your tasks, task notes, Knowledge and Notes are kept locally, and notes are plain Markdown files you can open in any editor. Flagship backs up your data before each update.

What happens if I stop paying for Pro?

You keep Flagship Free and nothing is deleted. One task's helper works at a time and one routine runs; the rest wait, untouched, until you upgrade again.
