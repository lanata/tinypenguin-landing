Astrotype Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

In short: what you type with Astrotype stays on your iPhone. There's no account. Your learned words and settings sync through your own iCloud, which we can't see. The app can send crash reports and anonymous counts to Sentry, never your text, and you can turn that off in Settings. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland it's off until you say yes. You only send us what you type if you choose to, in a problem report, and your word list only if you send it. We don't sell or share your data, and there are no ads or trackers.

Who we are

Tiny Penguin LLC, a California company in the United States, makes Astrotype. We're the controller of the personal data described here. Reach us at hello@tinypenguin.com about anything in this policy. Our main privacy policy covers our other apps and this website.

What stays on your iPhone

  • What you type. Astrotype works out your words on your iPhone. It never sends what you type in other apps to us or anyone else, except in a problem report you choose to send (below).
  • What the keyboard learns. Your learned words, word pairs, blocked words, text replacements, settings, and where your thumbs tend to land stay on your device.
  • iCloud sync. With Full Access on, your learned words and settings sync to your other devices through your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database). Your iPhone encrypts them before they leave, with a key kept in your iCloud Keychain, so neither we nor Apple can read them. A new device or a reinstall on the same Apple Account can still restore them.
  • The keyboard's last minute. So you can report a problem, the keyboard keeps a short history of its last minute in memory, and leaves it in the app's shared storage on your iPhone when it closes. The app ignores and deletes it after 3 minutes, and when you open Report an issue it's held in memory, not saved again. Nothing leaves your phone unless you tap Send in Report an issue.

What we collect, and why

Astrotype uses Sentry, run by Functional Software, Inc. in the United States, to receive the following. None of it is linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and none of it is used for advertising or tracking.

  • Crash reports and performance data, only with "Share anonymous usage data" on. Astrotype shows this switch during setup. It starts off if your iPhone or iPad is set to a region in the EU, EEA, UK or Switzerland, and on elsewhere, and you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy. They include: where the app or keyboard crashed, with your device model, iOS version, memory use and Astrotype's version. The keyboard sends these only with Full Access on, and they're built so they can't carry text: no logs, screenshots, typed words or crash messages.
  • Anonymous usage counts, with the same switch on: numbers such as whether the keyboard was turned on, a speed test finished, or the Pro trial started, and how many words went in as the keyboard's first guess. Counts only, never the words.
  • Problem reports, only when you tap Send in Report an issue: the reason you picked, your note, your device, iOS and Astrotype version, and, if you choose, a history of what you did on the keyboard over the last minute (or 5, 15 or 30 minutes with Keep recording on). With "Include what I typed" off, that history has only timing, key kinds and lengths, no letters or words. With it on, it includes your touches and what you typed, never in password or one-time-code fields.
  • Word lists you send us, only when you tap Send under "Send these words to us" at the end of Learned words or Never suggest: the words you left ticked, each with its language, and for learned words how many times you wrote it and whether you added or boosted it, plus the languages you use and Astrotype's version. We use them to add missing words to the dictionary, and take wrong ones out, for everyone.
  • Practice words, only with "Send practice sessions" on (off by default): words you type in the app's speed test and drills, one at a time, with their touches, the day and a random ID. With "Include corrections from other apps" also on, a word you fixed from the suggestion bar in another app, with the guess it replaced and its touches, and nothing around it. We use these to make Astrotype's guesses better.

Legal basis: your consent. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, everything above is off until you turn it on or tap Send. Elsewhere, usage data starts on and everything else starts off. You can turn each switch off in Settings at any time, and sending stops right away. Sentry doesn't store your IP address with reports.

Purchases

Apple sells Astrotype Pro through the App Store. Apple handles your payment under its own privacy policy; the app only learns whether you have Pro. We never see your card or your Apple Account.

Game Center

If you use the leaderboards, Apple's Game Center shares your typing speed (words per minute) and your Game Center name with other players, under Apple's privacy policy. It's optional.

Full Access

iOS lets a keyboard ask for Full Access. Astrotype types fully without it. With it on, the keyboard can sync through iCloud, play haptics, read whether you have Pro, and send crash reports and problem reports as described above. It doesn't use it to send what you type.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. Sentry processes the data above for us as a service provider. Apple provides iCloud, the App Store and Game Center as an independent controller.

How long we keep it

  • Crash reports and usage counts: 90 days, after which Sentry deletes them.
  • Problem reports: until we've looked into the problem, and 90 days at most, after which Sentry deletes them.
  • Word lists you send us: 90 days at most, after which Sentry deletes them. Words we add to the dictionary stay in it, without anything about who sent them.
  • Practice words: deleted from our server once we've used them to improve Astrotype's guesses, and after 30 days at the most. Email us to have yours deleted sooner; tell us roughly when you sent them.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. Because what we receive isn't linked to you, we may need details from you (like when you sent a report) to find it. Email hello@tinypenguin.com; we answer within 30 days. If you're in the EEA or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. Our main privacy policy has more on international transfers and California rights, which apply here too.

Children

Astrotype isn't meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the EEA and UK, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for material changes we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.
