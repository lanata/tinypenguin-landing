Two thumbs, one word.

Swipe typing is one long swipe per word with one finger. Astrotype lets both thumbs share the work, so each one only covers its half of the keys.

  1. 1

    Tap or swipe with one thumb

    A tap is a letter. A short swipe is a few letters. Use whichever comes naturally.

  2. 2

    Keep going with the other

    Switch thumbs in the middle of a word, or use both at once. Nothing is final yet.

  3. 3

    Press space

    Astrotype turns the pieces into the word you meant, and learns your words as you go.

Questions

Is it hard to learn?

Not at first: Astrotype works like a normal keyboard from the start, with taps and one-finger swipes. Two-thumb typing is a new habit, and the app's lessons and drills walk you through it. Most people feel the rhythm within a week.

Why does it ask for Full Access?

Only for extras: syncing your words and settings through your iCloud, haptics, and sending a problem report when you tap Send. The keyboard types fully without it, and what you type never leaves your iPhone.

Which iPhones and languages does it support?

Any iPhone on iOS 18 or later. It types in English and Spanish, and Pro can do both at once. The app's screens are in English for now.

I used Nintype. Will this feel familiar?

Yes. Astrotype is built around the same idea: both thumbs, taps and swipes mixed, and the word is only chosen when you press space. It's a fresh keyboard made for today's iPhones.

What happens when the trial ends?

You keep the free keyboard, with all your learned words and settings. Pro features wait for you until you subscribe.
