Astrotype Support

We read every message.

Something not right? In Astrotype, open the keyboard's menu (the Astrotype logo) or the app's Settings and choose Report an issue. It can include the last minute of what happened on the keyboard, which helps us fix it fast. Or email hello@stellorum.com.

Turn on the keyboard

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard and pick Astrotype.
  3. In any app, tap and hold the globe key (or the emoji key) and choose Astrotype.

The Astrotype app walks you through this the first time, and you can run it again from Settings → Run setup again.

Do I need Full Access?

No. The keyboard types fully without it. Turn it on if you want your learned words and settings to sync over iCloud, haptics, and to send problem reports. What you type stays on your iPhone either way. See the privacy policy.

How do I type with two thumbs?

Tap or swipe a few letters with one thumb, then carry on with the other, in the same word. The word is chosen when you press space. The app's Learn and practice screen teaches it step by step, and drills help it become a habit. Two-thumb typing is part of Astrotype Pro.

The keyboard picked the wrong word

  • Tap the word to see other guesses and swap it, or teach Astrotype a word it doesn't know.
  • Hold a guess on the bar to block a word you never want.
  • If it keeps happening, send a report with Report an issue and pick "Wrong word". It's the best way for us to fix it.

Astrotype Pro

  • Trial: every new install gets 14 days of Pro, no sign-up. After that you keep the free keyboard and all your words.
  • Lifetime: pay once and keep Pro for good, on the same Apple Account. Nothing renews.
  • Restore: on a new iPhone, open the Pro screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Astrotype Pro, or use Manage subscription in the app.
  • Refunds: Apple handles App Store purchases. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Pro doesn't show in the keyboard: open the Astrotype app once, and make sure Full Access is on so the keyboard can see your subscription.

Languages

Astrotype types English and Spanish, and every language is free. Pro can type both at once and tells them apart as you go. The app's screens are in English for now.

Still stuck?

Email hello@stellorum.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened. We usually answer within a couple of days.
