Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

In short: your code, projects and agent conversations stay on your Mac. We only keep what an account needs (an email address or Apple sign-in ID, and your plan), Paddle handles payments, and the app sends crash reports and usage analytics to Sentry that contain nothing about your work and can be turned off. We don't sell data or use ads.

Who we are

Tiny Penguin LLC ("we", "us") makes Flotilla and runs tinypenguin.com. Reach us at hello@tinypenguin.com about anything in this policy.

What stays on your Mac

Flotilla keeps your projects, sessions, settings, files, git checkouts and agent transcripts on your Mac. We never receive them. Pull request data comes from GitHub through the GitHub CLI you've signed in to, directly on your Mac.

Your account

If you sign in, our billing server stores:

  • your email address, or the identifier and email Apple shares when you use Sign in with Apple (which may be a private relay address);
  • your plan, trial and subscription status, and the dates they change.

We use this to sign you in, unlock Pro and send the sign-in emails you ask for. We keep it while your account exists and delete it when you ask us to.

Payments

Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for our orders, so Paddle collects your payment details, billing address and tax information and handles them under its own privacy policy. We receive order and subscription details from Paddle, such as your email, plan and country, but never your full card number.

Crash reports and usage analytics (Sentry)

Flotilla uses Sentry, run by Functional Software, Inc. in the United States, for crash reporting and usage analytics. Sentry processes this data for us under its privacy policy. It's on by default in released versions of Flotilla, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings with "Send crash reports and usage data". Sending stops as soon as you do.

What Flotilla sends:

  • Crash reports: the stack trace of Flotilla's code when it crashes, with device details such as your Mac model, macOS version and memory, and Flotilla's version. Your home folder path is replaced with ~, and the trail of recent actions is removed before sending.
  • Error reports: which place in Flotilla's code showed you an error, not the error's message.
  • Usage metrics: counts such as app launches (with how many projects and sessions you have, your default agent and which of a few settings are on), new sessions (which agent, which checkout mode, where it was started, whether it was forked), how many agents are running, and which kinds of pane tabs get opened.
  • An install ID: a random ID created on your Mac so we can tell installs apart. It isn't linked to your account or email.

Flotilla never sends your code, file contents, prompts, messages, agent transcripts, or the names of your projects, sessions, repos or branches. Like any web service, Sentry sees the IP address a report comes from.

We use this only to find and fix crashes and bugs and to learn which features people use. We don't combine it with your account, use it for advertising or sell it. Sentry keeps it for a limited period under our plan's retention settings and then deletes it. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping Flotilla working, and you can object at any time by turning it off.

Updates

Flotilla checks for and downloads updates from GitHub, where our releases are hosted. Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address when that happens.

Agents you run

Flotilla starts agent tools such as Claude Code, Codex and Cursor on your Mac. What you send through them goes straight to their providers under those providers' privacy policies, not through us.

This website

tinypenguin.com uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Flotilla page asks GitHub's API for the latest download link, so GitHub may log your IP address as part of serving those requests.

Sharing

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use it for advertising. We share it only with the providers named here (Paddle, Sentry, our email delivery and hosting providers) to run the Services, or when the law requires it.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Residents of the EU, UK and California have these rights by law. Email hello@tinypenguin.com and we'll answer within 30 days.

Children

The Services aren't meant for children under 13, or under 16 in the EU and UK, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and for material changes we'll let you know in the app or by email.
