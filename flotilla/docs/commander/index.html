The commander PRO

The commander is one agent above all your projects. Tell it what you want done and it starts agents in the right project, passes your messages on, and keeps track of it all, so you don't have to pick a project or open a new agent for every little thing.

Talk to the commander

The commander sits at the top of the Projects list in the sidebar, above every project. Click it, or press ⌥1: it's always first, so it's always ⌥1. It opens as the first pane too, at the top left of your agents. Then just ask:

  • "Fix the login crash from this morning's Sentry issues in the iOS app."
  • "Tell the checkout agent to use the new API instead."
  • "What's everyone working on? Anything waiting on me?"
  • "Where do we parse the config file in the web app?"

What it does

  • Starts agents: it picks the project, writes the agent a clear brief, and starts it. Related small tasks go to one agent together rather than one agent each.
  • Passes messages on: to any agent, saying they're from you.
  • Keeps track: agents it starts report back to it when they open a pull request, finish or get stuck, and it tells you what needs you.
  • Answers quick questions: it can read the code in all your projects, and use tools you've connected, like your issue tracker, without starting an agent for it.

The commander coordinates; agents do the work. It doesn't make commits or branches itself, so every change still happens in an agent you can follow, review and merge as usual.

It remembers

The commander keeps notes in its own folder: what's in flight, what happened the last few days, and what it has learned about you and your projects, like how you like things done. Tell it a preference once and it keeps it.

After a couple of idle hours it goes to sleep, and the next time you need it, it starts a fresh conversation from those notes. Its conversations stay short and quick without you having to manage them. With its pane closed it sleeps after 10 idle minutes instead, and wakes on the same conversation when you or an agent message it.

Right-click the commander and choose Start New Conversation to start fresh right away. ⌥W closes its pane and leaves it working; ⌥Q stops it, and it starts a new conversation the next time you open it.

Settings

Open Settings › Commander, or right-click the commander and choose Commander Settings…, to:

  • Turn the commander on or off. Off, it leaves the sidebar; its notes are kept for when you turn it back on.
  • Choose the agent it runs: Claude Code, Codex or Cursor.
  • Choose how much it does itself: Answer more itself, Balanced or Delegate almost everything.
  • Add your own instructions for it, like "Ask me before starting more than three agents."
  • Open Folder to see its notes, or Clear Memory… to have it forget everything.
