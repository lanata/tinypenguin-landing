Free and Pro

Flotilla is free to use, for good. Pro is for running a bigger fleet, and every account gets 14 days to try it.

What's free

  • Up to 3 agents running at once
  • 1 project and 1 routine
  • Claude Code, Codex and Cursor
  • The grid, live status and notifications
  • The Editor, Changes, Pull Requests and Terminal tabs, including merging pull requests
  • The flotilla command, so agents can work together

What Pro adds

No limitsRun as many agents, projects and routines as you like.
Agents that survive quittingQuit or update Flotilla and agents keep working. More
Permission rulesCommands agents may run without asking. More
Review commentsComment on an agent's diff, and on GitHub pull requests. More
AI reviewAn agent reviews pull requests, explains the tricky parts and answers your questions. More
Ready worktreesNew agents start instantly. More
Sleep idle agentsIdle agents free their memory. More
Keep the Mac awakeStep away while agents work. More
Session historyBring back deleted agents. More
Multi-repo projectsOne project across several repositories. More
Custom system promptYour instructions for every agent. More

Pro features are marked with a PRO badge in the app and in these docs.

Try it free

  1. Open Settings › Pro.
  2. Choose monthly ($10) or yearly ($100, two months free).
  3. Click Start 14-Day Free Trial. Checkout opens in your browser.

Cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged.

If you don't subscribe

You go back to Free, and nothing is deleted. Your first project and oldest routine keep working; anything past the free limits waits, locked, until you upgrade. Permission rules are set aside and come back with Pro.

Managing your subscription

  • Change or cancel: Settings › Pro › Manage Subscription…
  • On another Mac: click Restore Purchase… in Settings › Pro, enter your email, and open the link Flotilla sends you on that Mac.
  • Refunds: see the refund policy.
